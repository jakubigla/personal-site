# Design direction - Editorial Stage

Canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/G1fVTJT7cpMHcxukbTptFz (boards `direction` and
`direction-mobile`; round 1 options A, B and C beside them).

## The direction in plain words

A calm, senior, cinematic page. Each scroll step makes one big statement set in a serif
(Fraunces) beside one moving object, the way an editorial feature or a keynote stage works.
The dark theme is a warm near-black stage with ivory text and a single coral accent; the light
theme is warm ivory paper with ink text and a brick-red accent, following the system setting.
Body text, labels and buttons are a clean sans (Manrope). Corners are softly rounded (10 px),
shadows soft, spacing generous.

## Why it fits the brief

- Enterprise buyers must not read the page as a toy: the look is restrained and editorial, so
  the motion reads as craft, not decoration.
- It carries the brief's "The Last Boring Slide" techniques directly: the flat sheet that tilts
  back into a large dark space (Act 1), data that outgrows its frame (Act 3), and big calm
  statements one idea per step.
- It reads as a CV in 60 seconds: the role statement is the largest thing on screen and the
  calls to action sit right under it.

## What makes it distinctive

- The sheet: a physical card that tilts back in 3D and holds a chart whose last bar outgrows it.
- The constellation stays, but quiet: thin ivory lines at low opacity on dark, ink lines on
  paper in light, never labelled.
- Proof is stated in words (years, roles, the kind of work), never as counts of catalogs,
  datasets or certifications.
- Motion uses two curves: a soft standard ease for reveals and a slower in-out "stage" ease for
  the sheet and camera moves; durations stay between 150 and 900 ms, Act 1 excepted.

## Shaped by

- Round 1 options: A "Plexus Night" (the brief's navy, gradient and glass look), B "Drawing
  Office" (blueprint grid with a labelled governance diagram), C "Editorial Stage".
- Jakub picked C. He liked B, but found its identity, catalog and grant flow diagram cringe, so
  no labelled flow diagrams appear. He asked for no mention of the number of governed catalogs,
  datasets or certifications, so the proof strip states roles and experience in words.
