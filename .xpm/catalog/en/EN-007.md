## Outcome
Provide the Canvas 2D or SVG node graph that sits behind the page and carries the governance story: a drifting constellation of nodes and thin glowing edges that can be driven by the motion system into ordered layers (people and groups, catalogs, datasets) with access paths drawn one by one. It eases to rest within about 2 s when the visitor stops scrolling or moving the pointer, applies a few pixels of pointer parallax on desktop only, renders as ink on paper in the light theme, and shows a fully drawn static graph in the reduced variant.

## Acceptance
- AC1: The graph renders behind the skeleton page from a data set of invented node and layer names, and none of the names is a real client, catalog, domain or team name.
- AC2: Driving the graph's progress from 0 to 1 moves it from the loose drifting state to the ordered, layered state with access paths drawn.
- AC3: With no scroll or pointer input the graph comes to rest within about 2 s and stops requesting animation frames.
- AC4: In the reduced variant the graph is drawn complete and static.
- AC5: The graph is decorative to assistive technology (hidden from the accessibility tree), and text over it meets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both themes.
- AC6: Pointer parallax is applied on a fine pointer and not on touch.

The acts that use it beyond Act 2 depend on which acts survive the design gate (open decision).
