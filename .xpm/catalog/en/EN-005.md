## Outcome
Produce and get approved the look and feel every UI ticket builds on: the deep navy canvas, the gradient name and section accents, glass cards with hairline borders and tech pills, light and dark theme colour tokens that keep WCAG 2.2 AA contrast over the animated background, type scale, spacing, and the shared easing and duration tokens (150 ms to 900 ms, Act 1 excepted). The direction records the techniques taken from the inspiration references without copying any of their assets, copy or layout.

## Acceptance
- AC1: A design direction document exists in the repo and records the owner's approval at the design gate.
- AC2: Colour, type, spacing, radius and shadow tokens are defined for both light and dark themes, and every text-on-background pair listed meets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
- AC3: Easing curves and durations are defined as named tokens, none under 150 ms or over 900 ms except the Act 1 sequence.
- AC4: The tokens are available to the build as a single source (for example CSS custom properties) that the skeleton page consumes.
- AC5: Approved mockups exist for the hero, one work card and the mobile hero.
