## Capability
On first load the page opens as a faithful recreation of today's plain jacops.pl (three icons and the old title in default styling); an editor cursor selects the old title, deletes it and types the new headline, then the flat page tilts back in 3D, shrinks into one sheet floating in a dark navy space and the real hero assembles around it.

## Acceptance
- AC1: On a first visit in a browser session, the sequence plays once and lasts about 4 s.
- AC2: The sequence starts from a recreation of the current page: three icons and the title "JacOps - Cloud Native and Cyber Security" in default styling.
- AC3: A visible editor cursor selects the old title, deletes it and types the new headline.
- AC4: After the headline is typed, the page tilts back in 3D, shrinks into a single sheet in a dark navy space and the final hero assembles around it.
- AC5: Any scroll, key press or tap during the sequence skips it straight to the final hero state.
- AC6: On a repeat visit in the same browser session the sequence does not play and the page opens in the final hero state.
- AC7: The old "JacOps" title is visible for no more than about a second before it is rewritten and appears nowhere else outside the footer.

## Notes
Act 1 is drawn on top of the real hero HTML and must never delay it (motion budget and fallbacks are NFRs). Under prefers-reduced-motion Act 1 is skipped. Open decision 4: Act 1 is one of the minimum acts that must survive the design gate.
