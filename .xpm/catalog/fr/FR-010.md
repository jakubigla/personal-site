## Capability
A compact experience timeline lists the career entries newest first on a vertical centre line with a node dot per role; on desktop the cards alternate left and right, on mobile they sit in a single column.

## Acceptance
- AC1: The timeline lists every career entry from the content file, newest first.
- AC2: Each entry shows the employer or company, the years and a short description.
- AC3: On a desktop-width viewport the entry cards alternate left and right of a centre line, with a node dot on the line per role.
- AC4: On a 360 px wide viewport the entries show in a single column.
- AC5: With JavaScript off the full timeline, including the centre line, is visible.
