## Capability
A Selected work section shows 4 to 6 glass cards from the selected-work table, each with an anonymized label, years, the problem, what was built, outcome numbers and a row of tech pills.

## Acceptance
- AC1: The section shows between 4 and 6 cards, each read from the content file.
- AC2: Each card shows its anonymized label, years, problem, what was built, outcome numbers and a row of tech pills.
- AC3: Every card label is one of the default anonymized labels (for example "Large European retailer"); no card shows a real client name.
- AC4: No card states a country or region finer than "European", a sector finer than one word, counts of subsidiaries or business units, or internal codenames.
- AC5: Each card has a small icon tile top left and a translucent glass surface with a hairline border.

## Notes
Client naming rule: no client is named at launch; a real client name must never enter the repo, its history, commits, tickets or images. Never publish audit findings, security weaknesses, contract values or RFP content. If a combination of facts on one card would identify the client, drop a fact.
