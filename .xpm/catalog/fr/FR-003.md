## Capability
As the visitor scrolls past the hero, the loose, drifting constellation of nodes behind the page snaps into order: nodes sort into layers (people and groups, catalogs, datasets) and access paths draw from identity to data one by one, each lighting up as it is granted; the practice areas appear as labels on the graph.

## Acceptance
- AC1: Before the visitor scrolls past the hero, the background shows a loose constellation of nodes joined by thin glowing edges.
- AC2: Scrolling past the hero sorts the nodes into three visible layers: people and groups, catalogs, datasets.
- AC3: Access paths draw from identity nodes to data nodes one at a time as scroll progresses, and each path lights up when it is drawn.
- AC4: Scrolling back up reverses the progress; the state always matches the scroll position.
- AC5: The five practice areas from the content file appear as labels on the graph.
- AC6: Every node or label name on the graph comes from an invented list; none is a real catalog, domain, team or client name.

## Notes
Traceability rule: every catalog, domain, team or group name on the graph is invented, never a real client name. Open decision 4: Act 2 is one of the minimum acts. With reduced motion the graph shows fully drawn and static.
