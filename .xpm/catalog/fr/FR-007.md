## Capability
Selected work cards slide in along the graph edges as glass cards; hovering or focusing a card highlights the part of the background graph it relates to.

## Acceptance
- AC1: As the Selected work section enters the viewport, each card slides in along a graph edge.
- AC2: Hovering a card highlights the related nodes and edges of the background graph and shows a soft inner glow on the card.
- AC3: Focusing a card with the keyboard produces the same graph highlight as hovering it.
- AC4: Which part of the graph a card relates to is defined in the content file.
