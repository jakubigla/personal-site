## Capability
A living constellation of nodes and thin glowing edges sits behind every section on a deep navy canvas; it drifts only while the visitor scrolls or moves the pointer, then eases to rest, and on desktop it follows the pointer with a subtle parallax of a few pixels.

## Acceptance
- AC1: The constellation is visible behind every section.
- AC2: The constellation drifts while the visitor scrolls or moves the pointer and eases to rest within about 2 s after input stops.
- AC3: On a desktop pointer device the constellation shifts by a few pixels with pointer movement.
- AC4: On a touch device there is no pointer parallax.

## Notes
Priority not set: the brief does not rank this item. Pausing when hidden or off screen and the low-power fallback are motion-budget NFRs.
