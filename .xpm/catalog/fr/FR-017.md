## Capability
Each act maps to a section id, so a URL hash such as #work or #timeline opens the page at that section in the final state of its act, and returns in the same state when shared.

## Acceptance

- AC2: Opening the page with a section hash scrolls to that section and shows its act in the final state, with no partial animation.
- AC3: Opening a section hash skips Act 1.
- AC4: The browser back and forward buttons keep working with native scroll; no scroll is hijacked.
