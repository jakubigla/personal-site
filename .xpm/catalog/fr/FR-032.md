## Capability
Jakub sees how many visits the site gets, through a cookieless counter (CloudFront logs or a privacy-first service).

## Acceptance
- AC1: Jakub can see a visit count per day.
- AC2: The counter sets no cookie and needs no consent banner.
- AC3: The counter loads no script from an external origin at runtime.

## Notes
Analytics that set cookies are out of scope; no third-party scripts from external origins at runtime.
