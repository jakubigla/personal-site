## Capability
In the proof strip, one scale figure (for example datasets governed or streams run) is drawn as a small line or bar that keeps growing past the edge of its card; the card stacks a second and third panel to hold it, then settles. The other numbers count up once.

## Acceptance
- AC1: When the proof strip enters the viewport, the chosen figure's line or bar grows past the edge of its card.
- AC2: The card stacks a second and then a third panel to hold the growing figure, then the stack settles.
- AC3: Every other numeric item counts up from zero to its final value exactly once per page view.
- AC4: Which figure breaks out is set in the content file, not in layout code.
- AC5: The final value of every number is present in the HTML before any animation runs.

## Notes
Priority not set: Needs (Must) 2 asks for the breakout figure, but open decision 4 says Acts 3 to 6 may ship as plain reveals if the budget is tight. With reduced motion or JavaScript off, counts show final values and the chart shows its final state.
