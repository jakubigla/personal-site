## Capability
The timeline's centre line draws itself with scroll progress, each role node lights as the line passes it, and the line's gradient shifts from the early-career colour to the current one.

## Acceptance
- AC1: The drawn length of the centre line follows the scroll position through the timeline section, forwards and backwards.
- AC2: Each role node lights up when the drawn line reaches it.
- AC3: The line's gradient moves from the early-career colour at the oldest entry to the current colour at the newest.
- AC4: Loading the page at #timeline shows the line fully drawn with every node lit.

Priority not set: Needs (Must) 5 asks for a line that draws itself as you scroll, but open decision 4 says Acts 3 to 6 may ship as plain reveals if the budget is tight.
