## Constraint
The Databricks Champion badge image is used only from the official credential issued to Jakub and is linked to its verification page; no badge copied from another website and no redrawn Databricks logo or badge is ever used, and the portrait placeholder is a generated on-brand graphic, never a stock photo or a generated face.

## Acceptance
- AC1: Until the official badge file is present in assets, the page ships with Champion status as text only and no badge image.
- AC2: When the badge is shown, it is the issued credential file and links to its verification page on the credential issuer's site.
- AC3: No Databricks logo or badge is drawn in SVG, CSS or canvas anywhere in the site.
- AC4: The portrait placeholder is an initials or abstract graphic; no stock photograph or AI-generated face is in the repo.

## Notes
The exact credential name is an open decision for Jakub.
