## Constraint
When the visitor sets prefers-reduced-motion, Act 1 is skipped, the graph is shown fully drawn and static, counts show final values, and section reveals become simple opacity changes or none; all content stays available.

## Acceptance
- AC1: With prefers-reduced-motion set, the Act 1 opening sequence does not play and the page loads in its final hero state.
- AC2: With prefers-reduced-motion set, the constellation graph renders fully drawn and does not move, including on scroll and pointer movement.
- AC3: With prefers-reduced-motion set, counters show their final values immediately.
- AC4: With prefers-reduced-motion set, no transform-based motion (translate, scale, rotate, 3D tilt, parallax) runs; reveals use opacity only or nothing.
- AC5: An automated test emulates reduced motion and asserts AC1 to AC4.

## Notes
The same reduced variant is the target of the low-power fallback (NFR-006).
