## Constraint
No fonts, scripts, styles or other assets are loaded from external origins at runtime; everything the page needs is served from the site's own origin.

## Acceptance
- AC1: All fonts, scripts, styles and images referenced by the built page resolve to the site's own origin.
- AC2: An automated test loads the built page and asserts that no network request goes to another origin (outbound navigation links excluded).
- AC3: The Content-Security-Policy enforces same-origin for script, style, font, image and connect sources.

## Notes
Outbound links (LinkedIn, GitHub, the credential verification page, the public repository) are navigations, not runtime loads, and are allowed.
