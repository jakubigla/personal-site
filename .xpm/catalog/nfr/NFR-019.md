## Constraint
At expected traffic the hosting costs under 2 USD per month: no always-on compute, no NAT gateway and no WAF plan.

## Acceptance
- AC1: The Terraform code provisions no always-on compute, no NAT gateway and no WAF web ACL or plan.
- AC2: A written cost estimate for the provisioned resources at expected traffic (S3, CloudFront, Route 53, ACM) totals under 2 USD per month.
- AC3: A policy check in CI fails a change that adds one of the excluded resource types.

## Notes
The Route 53 hosted zone already exists in Jakub's account.
