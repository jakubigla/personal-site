## Constraint
Jakub is told by email when the monthly AWS cost reaches 80% or 100% of the 2 USD cap, and when a deploy to the live site fails. No uptime check is provisioned.

## Acceptance
- AC1: Terraform defines an AWS Budgets monthly cost budget of 2 USD with email notifications to Jakub at 80% (1.60 USD) and 100% (2.00 USD) of actual spend.
- AC2: A deploy run on main that fails at any step sends an email to Jakub naming the failed run.
- AC3: No uptime check, synthetic probe or scheduled health-check resource is provisioned.
- AC4: The alerting resources keep the total hosting cost under 2 USD per month (NFR-019).

## Notes
The PO chose no uptime check: a static site behind a CDN rarely goes down, and a probe adds cost against NFR-019. Budget alerts arrive hours late, so they warn rather than cap. GitHub Actions' built-in failure notification to the workflow's actor may meet AC2 if it reliably reaches Jakub for merges to main; architecture decides. The PO did not place this in the MVP; it can ride along with the hosting enabler.
