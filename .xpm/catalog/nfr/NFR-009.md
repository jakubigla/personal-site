## Constraint
All easing curves and durations come from one shared set of design tokens, and no transition is faster than 150 ms or slower than 900 ms except the Act 1 sequence; pointer parallax of the constellation is a few pixels on desktop and absent on touch.

## Acceptance
- AC1: Easing curves and durations are defined once as tokens and every animation references them; no hard-coded durations or curves outside the token file.
- AC2: A check over the token set confirms every duration is between 150 ms and 900 ms, with Act 1 the only named exception.
- AC3: Pointer parallax is enabled only for fine pointers with hover (for example the media query pointer fine and hover hover) and moves the constellation by no more than a few pixels.
- AC4: On touch devices no parallax runs.

## Notes
Motion must tell the story of the content (governance, scale, delivery), never be decoration for its own sake; that judgement sits with the design gate.
