## Constraint
The MVP ships the AWS Budgets alert of NFR-032 (email at 80% and 100% of the 2 USD monthly cap), defined in Terraform, and every site response is cacheable at the CloudFront edge so a request flood is served from cache and origin requests stay near zero; no WAF is added (NFR-019).

## Acceptance
- AC1: Terraform defines an AWS Budgets monthly cost budget of 2 USD with email notifications at 80% and 100% of actual spend, to Jakub's address.
- AC2: HTML, hashed assets and every other site object are served with Cache-Control headers that let CloudFront cache them; a repeat request for any page or asset is a CloudFront cache hit.
- AC3: No AWS WAF web ACL or other paid protection resource is defined (NFR-019 holds).
- AC4: Negative: removing the budget or its notifications from Terraform fails the static policy check in CI.

## Notes
Mitigates T-2 (infra / CloudFront hosting): a request or bandwidth flood could push cost past the 2 USD cap with no WAF allowed. Pulls NFR-032's budget alert into the MVP. autopilot decisions batch, Q20.
