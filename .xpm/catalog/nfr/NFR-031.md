## Constraint
CloudFront standard and real-time access logging stay off at launch, so the site owner keeps no visitor IP addresses or user agents and the footer "no cookies, no tracking" privacy note stays true. Logging may be turned on only by FR-032, under its conditions.

## Acceptance
- AC1: The Terraform for the CloudFront distribution configures no standard access logging, no real-time log configuration and no log delivery destination.
- AC2: No log bucket, Kinesis stream or other store for CloudFront request logs exists in the Terraform code at launch.
- AC3: A CI policy check fails a change that enables CloudFront standard or real-time logging unless the same change implements FR-032 AC5 to AC7 (IP minimisation, lifecycle expiry and the updated footer privacy note).
- AC4: The footer privacy note (FR-021) makes no claim that would become untrue while logging is off, and NFR-016 AC4 holds.

## Notes
Logging is off at launch. The two answers fit together over time: Q4 sets logging off at launch, and Q9 keeps the visit counter (FR-032) as a Could after launch. If that counter is ever built, it turns logging on only with minimised IPs and short retention, and it updates the privacy note in the same change. CloudFront still handles visitor IPs briefly to serve requests, but the site owner stores none. Leaving logging off also keeps log storage out of the NFR-019 budget. mvp: true because the brief's Must footer privacy note (Needs Must 13, NFR-016 AC4) states no tracking.
