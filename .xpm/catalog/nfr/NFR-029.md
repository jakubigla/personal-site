## Constraint
A first-time visitor arriving from a LinkedIn post understands the current profile, sees the proof and knows how to make contact within 60 seconds, and the page also reads as a CV in that time even for visitors who are not the target audience.

## Acceptance
- AC1: The first screen at 360 px and at desktop width shows the name, the role headline, the Champion status and the primary contact call to action without scrolling (after Act 1 ends or is skipped).
- AC2: Section order puts proof (proof strip and selected work) before the long-form sections.
- AC3: A reviewer timing a cold read of the page finds current role, key credentials, proof figures and contact routes within 60 seconds.

## Notes
The link preview and the first screen matter most because the site launches with a LinkedIn post.
