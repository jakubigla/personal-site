## Constraint
On low-power devices (low hardware concurrency or device memory, or frame time over budget for about a second) the page drops to the reduced-motion variant automatically, without user action.

## Acceptance
- AC1: When hardware concurrency or device memory reports below the thresholds set in the motion system, the page starts in the reduced variant.
- AC2: When measured frame time stays over the frame budget for about one second during animation, the page switches to the reduced variant and stays there for the rest of the visit.
- AC3: The thresholds and frame budget are named constants in one place, not scattered literals.
- AC4: The switch keeps the current scroll position and does not hide or reflow content.

## Notes
The brief does not fix the thresholds or the frame budget; the architecture or design stage sets them.
