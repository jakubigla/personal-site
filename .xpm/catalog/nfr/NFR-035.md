## Constraint
Every DNS record for the apex and www names is managed in Terraform and points only at a CloudFront distribution and bucket this stack owns, so retiring the old site never leaves a record another account could claim.

## Acceptance
- AC1: Terraform defines the apex and www alias records (A and AAAA) to the new distribution, in the existing zone referenced as data
- AC2: The cut-over runbook switches both records to the new distribution and verifies them before the old distribution, bucket or records are deleted, and lists every old record to remove
- AC3: Negative: after the runbook's retire step, no record in the zone points at a CloudFront distribution or S3 website endpoint outside this stack's Terraform state

## Notes
Mitigates T-9 (infra). The old site's resources are retired after the attended cut-over (ADR-0008, NFR-022).
