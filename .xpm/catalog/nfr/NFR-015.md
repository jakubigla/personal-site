## Constraint
No real client name ever enters the repository, its history, commit messages, tickets or image files, not even behind a flag; nothing on the page lets a reader work out which company a fact belongs to; and no audit findings, security weaknesses, contract values or RFP content of any client is published.

## Acceptance
- AC1: Every selected-work card uses an anonymized label only; no client name appears anywhere in the repo, its history, commit messages, PRs or tickets.
- AC2: No location finer than "European" and no sector finer than one word appears on any card.
- AC3: No counts of subsidiaries or business units, and no internal project, product, platform, catalog, domain or team names appear; all names on the hero graph are invented.
- AC4: No audit finding, security weakness, contract value or RFP content of any client is published; the pre-sales result line names no client and no figure.
- AC5: A CI check fails a pull request when it adds any term from a denylist kept outside the public repository (for example a CI secret or a local-only file), so the denylist itself never exposes a name.
- AC6: Each card's combination of facts is reviewed against the rule before merge; if a combination would identify the client, a fact is dropped.

## Notes
A client name may be added later only by Jakub, one client at a time, once cleared. That is a manual owner action outside the automated pipeline.
