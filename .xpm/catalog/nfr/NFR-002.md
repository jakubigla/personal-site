## Constraint
The hero text is real HTML, painted in the first frame and is the page's LCP element; the Act 1 opening sequence and every other animation are drawn on top of or after the content and never delay its paint.

## Acceptance
- AC1: The hero name, role and value statement are present as text in the initial HTML response, not injected by script.
- AC2: A performance trace of a first load shows the hero text element as the LCP element.
- AC3: Act 1's overlay is drawn in the first frame on top of the hero, which paints beneath it as real HTML and stays the LCP element; Act 1 never delays the hero's paint, and disabling the Act 1 script does not change the LCP time by more than measurement noise.
- AC4: No render-blocking script is needed to show any section's content.

## Notes
Pairs with NFR-001 (LCP budget) and NFR-004 (JavaScript off).
