## Constraint
The page works from 360 px viewport width upward and the mobile layout is designed, not merely shrunk: no horizontal scroll, no clipped content, and layouts such as the timeline switch to a single column.

## Acceptance
- AC1: At 360 px width no section causes horizontal page scroll.
- AC2: At 360 px width all text, cards, pills and links are fully visible and legible without zoom.
- AC3: The experience timeline renders as a single column on mobile and alternates left and right on desktop.
- AC4: Automated screenshot or layout checks run at 360 px, a tablet width and a desktop width.
