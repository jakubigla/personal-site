## Constraint
Nothing loops forever: the constellation drifts only while the visitor scrolls or moves the pointer and eases to rest within about 2 s, and all animation pauses while the tab is hidden or the animated element is off screen.

## Acceptance
- AC1: With no scroll or pointer input, no animation frame callback or CSS animation runs for longer than about 2 s after the last input.
- AC2: When the tab is hidden (visibilitychange), all requestAnimationFrame loops and canvas drawing stop until the tab is visible again.
- AC3: An animated element outside the viewport does no per-frame work.
- AC4: At the contact section (Act 6) the constellation comes to stillness.

## Notes
Supports NFR-001 (performance) and battery use on phones.
