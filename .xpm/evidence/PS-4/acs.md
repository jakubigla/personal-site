# PS-4 acceptance criteria

Source: taken from the ticket as written.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC1: A design direction document exists in the repo and records the owner's approval at the design gate. ([EN-005](https://github.com/jakubigla/personal-site/blob/main/.xpm/catalog/en/EN-005.md) AC1)
- AC2: Colour, type, spacing, radius and shadow tokens are defined for both light and dark themes, and every text-on-background pair listed meets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. ([EN-005](https://github.com/jakubigla/personal-site/blob/main/.xpm/catalog/en/EN-005.md) AC2)
- AC3: Easing curves and durations are defined as named tokens, none under 150 ms or over 900 ms except the Act 1 sequence. ([EN-005](https://github.com/jakubigla/personal-site/blob/main/.xpm/catalog/en/EN-005.md) AC3)
- AC4: The tokens are available to the build as a single source (for example CSS custom properties) that the skeleton page consumes. ([EN-005](https://github.com/jakubigla/personal-site/blob/main/.xpm/catalog/en/EN-005.md) AC4)
- AC5: Approved mockups exist for the hero, one work card and the mobile hero. ([EN-005](https://github.com/jakubigla/personal-site/blob/main/.xpm/catalog/en/EN-005.md) AC5)
